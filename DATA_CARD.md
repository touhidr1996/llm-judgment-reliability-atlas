# Data card

## Provenance

All events are created locally by `judgment_atlas.data.generate_judgments`. No paper dataset, human annotation, private prompt, proprietary model output, or personal record is copied. The generator is an independent simulation inspired by broad mechanisms described in the cited literature.

## Structure

Six thousand fictional events vary semantic context, simulated judge family, abstract persona, intervention, lexical overlap, ambiguity, and distance from a policy boundary. Labels and disagreements are sampled from an explicit seeded mechanism. Model names are descriptive fictional labels, not benchmarks of real systems.

## Appropriate use

Suitable for portfolio demonstration, unit tests, and method prototyping. It cannot support claims about any real model, user population, protected group, search system, or deployed decision process.
